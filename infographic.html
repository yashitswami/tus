Option A · Stepped Progression

The Eight-Year Journey

A linear educational progression where foundational mastery and campus enablers (05 & 06) directly prepare students for their Grade 11–12 specialization tracks (07 & 08).

01
Stage 01 · Grade 5–6

Laying Strong Foundations

The middle school journey begins with establishing deep conceptual understanding across every subject. During these formative years, students build strong foundations in Mathematics, Science, English and the Humanities while developing the habits that underpin lifelong learning.

  • ✓Early introduction to Algebra & Geometry fluency.
  • ✓25% classroom time devoted to physical laboratory investigation.
  • ✓Building work organization, effective communication, and collaborative inquiry.
02
Stage 02 · Grade 7–8

Deepening Understanding and Discovering Potential

As students become more confident learners, the curriculum expands both in depth and complexity. Scientific learning becomes increasingly inquiry-driven through laboratory investigations, experiments, observations, and research projects that strengthen conceptual understanding.

  • ✓Advanced Mathematics: Trigonometry, Quadratic Equations, Functions, Sequences.
  • ✓Exposure to competition-level problem sets (IOQM, Olympiads).
  • ✓Structured Life Skills: resilience, leadership, and emotional intelligence.
03
Stage 03 · Grade 9–10

Building Mastery

Between 50% and 85% of the high school curriculum has already been introduced in earlier grades, allowing students to focus on refining concepts, strengthening application skills and developing examination strategies rather than learning entirely new material under time pressure.

  • ✓Cambridge IGCSE Extended Board examinations mastery.
  • ✓November Board Series creates the crucial 6-month head-start before Grade 11.
  • ✓Gradual introduction to SAT, JEE, and NEET analytical problem-solving.
04
Stage 04 · Grade 11–12

Integrated Coaching with NV Classes

The final two years are designed to prepare students for College Entrance exams for Engineering and Medical streams with maximum school-day efficiency.

  • •Maximized School Hours: Direct synthesis between board syllabi and competitive test prep.
  • •Integrated Laboratory Sessions: Practical lab work synced with JEE/NEET concepts rather than isolated board routines.
  • •Curated Resource Library: High-value reference volumes readily borrowable without external expense.
  • •Executive Mentorship Workshops: Periodic sessions on Learning How to Learn, EQ, and Time Management.
05
Enabler 05 · Campus Infrastructure

05 / ECOSYSTEM: Built to Promote Intense Learning

A focused physical and digital campus environment designed to empower independent scholars to optimize every learning hour.

  • ✓Study Pods & Library: Dedicated silent seating zones to encourage deep self-learning and daily preparation.
  • ✓Audio-Visual Mediums: Full access to digital lecture archives and specialized video breakdowns.
06
Enabler 06 · Holistic Well-Being

06 / ENRICHMENT: Holistic Student Life & Co-Curriculars

Engage students in extracurriculars and sports matching their passion, ensuring scholars remember and cherish their senior school years.

  • ✓Athletic & Sports Tournaments: Competitive and recreational leagues fostering physical resilience.
  • ✓Expeditions & Culture: School trips, educational screenings, and performing arts festivals.
07/8
Stream 07 · National Route

For PU Batch

Tailored for scholars targeting national engineering and medical seats (JEE / NEET).

  • •Minimal classes for Hindi, English, and Computers; comprehensive notes provided.
  • •No redundant/duplicate PCM lectures; class time focused entirely on entrance rigour.
  • •PU internal assessments synced with NV Classes curriculum.
  • •Saves 6+ hours every week for concentrated entrance prep.
Stream 08 · Global Route

For IGCSE / Cambridge Batch

Designed for scholars aiming for international degree articulation and global admission.

  • •English assessed exclusively in Grade 12, preserving junior bandwidth.
  • •Board examinations completed by the November series.
  • •All advanced Cambridge topics covered seamlessly within school coaching.
