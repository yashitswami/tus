Our Curriculum

Four pillars, built to compound over eight years.

Accredited with Cambridge IGCSE, we use its rigorous framework as a foundation and enrich it with carefully selected elements from CBSE, ICSE and other leading curricula — combining academic rigor with experiential, project-based learning.

01 IGCSE Extended

Academic depth through the Cambridge IGCSE Extended curriculum

We prepare every student for the more rigorous Extended curriculum — Mathematics 0580 Extended, and extended Science papers that demand analytical thinking and higher-order application. This equips students to confidently meet the intellectual challenges of university education and competitive entrance examinations.

02 Early Start

Key concepts introduced early, for an unhurried path to mastery

Algebra begins in Grade 5, giving students years to build fluency instead of learning it under time pressure. Trigonometry is introduced in Grade 7, so it arrives as a familiar tool rather than an intimidating new topic in Grade 10 — reducing academic stress in the senior years.

03 Integrated Prep

A strong foundation for college entrance examinations

Preparing students for JEE, NEET, SAT, CUET and CLAT is integrated into school learning — not left to external coaching institutes. Students receive comprehensive preparation as part of their school day, while maintaining a healthy balance between academic excellence and well-being.

04 Olympiads

Preparing students for Olympiads and academic excellence

Our curriculum incorporates the knowledge and skills required for NSEJS, IOQM, RMO, NSEP, NSEC, IMO and other recognised competitions — developing analytical thinking, perseverance, and a strong academic profile, with personalised guidance for students who show aptitude and interest.

Progression Map

Every stage builds the foundation for the next.

Rather than treating each grade as an isolated milestone, our curriculum follows a carefully designed progression — building knowledge, skills, confidence and independence over eight transformative years.

STAGE 01 · GRADES 5–6

Laying Strong Foundations

Deep conceptual understanding across every subject. Students build fluency in arithmetic, language and scientific observation, and are introduced to algebra, geometry, logical reasoning, lab investigations and structured research projects — while learning to organise, communicate and collaborate.

  • ✓ Early introduction to Algebra & Geometry
  • ✓ 25% Classroom time in hands-on Science Labs
  • ✓ Structured inquiry and collaborative communication
STAGE 02 · GRADES 7–8

Deepening Understanding, Discovering Potential

The curriculum expands in depth and complexity — Trigonometry, Quadratic Equations, Functions and Sequences, alongside inquiry-driven science. Students are exposed to competition-level problem solving, and those with exceptional aptitude receive additional mentoring.

  • ✓ Trigonometry & Quadratic Equations introduced as familiar tools
  • ✓ Exposure to competition-level problem sets (IOQM, NSEJS)
  • ✓ Inquiry-driven experiments with quantitative analysis
STAGE 03 · GRADES 9–10

Building Mastery

By Grade 9, 50–85% of the high-school curriculum has already been introduced — so students refine concepts and exam strategy rather than learn entirely new material under pressure, while being gradually introduced to SAT, JEE and NEET-level thinking.

  • ✓ 50–85% of high-school syllabus pre-mastered without rush
  • ✓ Cambridge IGCSE Extended Board examinations mastery
  • ✓ Gradual bridge to JEE / NEET / SAT analytical depth
STAGE 04 · GRADES 11–12

Preparing for the World Beyond School

Students choose Science, Commerce or Humanities pathways, combining board exam preparation with JEE, NEET, CUET, CLAT and SAT coaching — plus personalised mentoring, career counselling, university application guidance and interview preparation.

  • ✓ Integrated entrance coaching in the regular school day
  • ✓ Global university admissions & portfolio mentorship
  • ✓ Career counseling, interview prep, and life skills execution
