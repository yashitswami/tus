IGCSE vs CBSE Framework

An objective, professional comparison designed to help parents select the learning structure best suited to their student's strengths and educational goals.

Comparison Area IGCSE CBSE
Subject Structure Linear framework with core foundational subjects and specific specialized electives. Standardized national curriculum framework across all central boards.
Organization of Subjects Focused study with flexible tiered levels tailored to student capability. Prescribed subject bundles with predetermined national grouping.
Curricular Focus Critical inquiry, practical application, data interpretation, and analytical writing. Comprehensive theoretical grounding with high alignment to national Indian entrance testing.
Flexibility High flexibility in choosing elective combinations (e.g., Economics alongside core sciences). Stream-based groupings (Science/Commerce/Arts) with fixed subject boundaries.
Practical Component Direct lab assessment, measurement technique verification, and experimental coursework. Practical lab exams conducted per internal and external board schemes.
Learning Approach Student-centered investigations, evaluation of evidence, and continuous analytical reasoning. Syllabus-driven mastery, strong textbook recall, and extensive cumulative testing.

Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does The Unbound School conduct IGCSE exams in November?

Completing the IGCSE board examinations in November allows Grade 11 to begin in February. This provides students with a crucial 6-month academic head start before their advanced secondary or entrance studies get underway.

Can a student enroll without an integrated coaching stream in 11th & 12th?

No. All students must select an integrated coaching stream (SAT Coaching or JEE/NEET Coaching). Schooling without integrated coaching is not available.

Are lab practicals included in internal evaluations?

Yes. Practicals are a central pillar of instruction at The Unbound School, involving physical instruments such as vernier calipers, screw gauges, and full chemical analysis benches.
